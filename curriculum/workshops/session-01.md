# Workshop Session 1 — Mental model, deep research & instructions

**Modules:** 1–3 (`mental-model`, `deep-research`, `system-instructions`)  
**Duration:** 90 minutes  
**Outcome:** Learners explain the loop, leave with a cited research pack, and tighten instructions from evidence.

## Agenda

| Minutes | Activity |
|--------:|----------|
| 0–20 | Mental model + messy-ask demo |
| 20–55 | Deep research → BRIEF.md / SOURCES.md (Firecrawl or equivalent) |
| 55–80 | System instructions drafted from the brief |
| 80–90 | Share-outs & take-home |

## Take-home

Complete Modules 1–3; finish BRIEF.md + SOURCES.md; bring the pack to Session 2.
