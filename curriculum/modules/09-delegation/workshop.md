# Workshop facilitator notes: Delegation and shared workspaces

**Workshop session:** 3
**Module duration:** ~35 minutes
**Closes Session 3**

## Purpose

Learners map agent layers, write workspace contracts, and repair unsafe parallel work before it spreads.

## Timing

| Segment | Minutes | Activity |
|---------|---------|----------|
| Teach | 6 | Model, agent, harness, framework, workspace. |
| Exercise 1 | 6 | Map one familiar tool into four layers. |
| Exercise 2 | 8 | Write one workspace and delegation contract. |
| Exercise 3 | 12 | Repair a shared-workspace collision. |
| Close | 3 | Recap: shrink, persist, split, share. |

## Demo steps

1. Map one familiar product into model, agent, harness, and optional framework.
2. Show the shared sources, rules, tools, approvals, logs, and handoff.
3. Split live into research vs writing briefs.
4. Show a collision: stale memory, old rules, and two editors.
5. Repair it with canonical sources, separate outputs, approval, and one merge owner.

## Discussion prompts

- "When did parallel agents help vs hurt?"
- "Which harness check would block your current team?"
- "Who is merge owner on your team today?"
- "What belongs in shared truth versus personal scratch?"
- "How does delegation interact with approvals (preview Module 10)?"

## Session 3 close

Four lenses on context: **shrink**, **persist**, **split**, and **share**. Preview Session 4: human craft and verify.

## Materials

- Four-layer map
- Workspace and delegation contract checklist
- Collision scenario
- Module diagram
