# Workshop facilitator notes: Human craft

**Workshop session:** 4  
**Module duration:** ~35 minutes  
**Opens Session 4**

## Purpose

Learners practice specs, reverse prompting, and mode/approval choices—putting human judgment at intent and verify nodes.

## Timing

| Segment | Minutes | Activity |
|---------|---------|----------|
| Teach | 10 | Spec sections; one-task focus; approval matrix intro. |
| Exercise 1 | 10 | Vague → one-page spec; pairs critique criteria. |
| Exercise 2 | 7 | Reverse prompting openers—read aloud. |
| Exercise 3 | 8 | Mode + approval for four tasks—debate risk. |
| Bridge | 2 | Preview verify: specs mean nothing without checks. |

## Demo steps

1. Run vague ask without spec—show fast wrong scope.
2. Re-run with acceptance criteria and "stop after plan."
3. Reverse prompt live—surface two interpretations before tools.
4. Show approval dialog or diff review—where gate sits in loop.

## Discussion prompts

- "What does your team always approve before vs after?"
- "When is autonomous mode irresponsible in your role?"
- "What's one acceptance criterion you will add tomorrow?"
- "How do playbooks (Module 4) differ from per-task specs?"

## Materials

- Vague request slide (Exercise 1)
- Sample approval matrix template
- Module diagram (intent + verify highlighted)
